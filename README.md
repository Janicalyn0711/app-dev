# Fast and Furious

> *"I don't have friends, I got family."*

**The Fast and the Furious** is an action-packed film franchise centered around *street racing*, *heists*, and *family loyalty*.

---

### Description
Fast and Furious is a high-octane action film franchise that revolves around illegal street racing, high-stakes heists, and the unbreakable bond of family. Led by Dominic Toretto and his crew, the series takes audiences on thrilling adventures across the globe, featuring incredible stunt work, customized cars, and unforgettable characters who view each other not just as friends, but as family.

### Main Characters

1. **Dominic Toretto** - The skilled street racer and leader of the crew.
2. **Brian O'Conner** - Former LAPD officer who becomes Dom's loyal partner and brother.
3. **Letty Ortiz** - Dom's fearless partner and a top-tier street racer.
4. **Mia Toretto** - Dom's sister and Brian's loving partner, providing vital support for the crew's operations.
5. **Roman Pearce** - The vocal and comedic member of the team who brings humor while handling high-speed drives.
